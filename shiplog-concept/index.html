01 / The problem

A customer rarely tells you everything in one place.

Usage can fall while support requests rise. A renewal can get closer while the account looks fine in a weekly report. The clues are there, but your team has to connect them.

01 — THE CHANGE

Something feels different

Usage drops, a key user disappears or a new team starts adopting the product.

02 — THE CONTEXT

The reason is somewhere else

Support history, account notes and product activity each explain a different part of the story.

03 — THE RESPONSE

Someone still has to connect it

Shiplog is designed to help your team see the pattern and decide which conversation to have next.

02 / How Shiplog helps

Less time piecing reports together. More time with the customer.

Ada helps make sense of the signals around an account, so your team can see what may be changing and choose a useful next step.

01 / Understand⌁

Bring the details together

Bring customer information from the tools you use into one place, so changes are easier to spot in context.

02 / Decide↗

Spot the change that matters

See which accounts may need attention, what has changed and why it could matter.

03 / Act→

Give the team a place to start

Turn a signal into a useful follow-up for the person who owns the relationship. Your team decides what happens next.
